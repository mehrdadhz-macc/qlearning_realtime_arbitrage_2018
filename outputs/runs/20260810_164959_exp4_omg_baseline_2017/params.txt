Experiment 4: OMG baseline, held-out 2017

Runs the deterministic OMG baseline (src/omg_baseline.py, Qin et al. 2016,
Wang & Zhang Sec. IV-C's comparison) directly on the 2017 held-out year --
kept separate from Experiments 1-3, which are all about the Q-learning
agent's seed-to-seed variance. OMG has no seed: its only "fitting" step is
a one-time causal calibration of [p_min, p_max] from the first
720 hours of data/train/isone_rt_hourly_lmp_2016.csv (not iterative
training -- see src/omg_baseline.py's module docstring), so a single run
is the complete, reproducible result.

Parameters
----------
train_data (calibration only): data/train/isone_rt_hourly_lmp_2016.csv
test_data (rolled out):        data/test/isone_rt_hourly_lmp_2017.csv
bin_calibration_hours:         720
capacity_mwh:                  8.0
max_rate_mw:                   [1.0, 2.0]
efficiency_charge:             1.0
efficiency_discharge:          1.0

Results
-------
1 MW: held-out 2017 profit = $10,841.02  (p_min=-155.44, p_max=360.00, W=0.0116405, Gamma=-5.19059)
2 MW: held-out 2017 profit = $8,753.44  (p_min=-155.44, p_max=360.00, W=0.00776036, Gamma=-4.79373)

See held_out_2017_omg_baseline_plot.png for the cumulative profit curves.
